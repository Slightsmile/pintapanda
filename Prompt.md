Create a beautiful, playful, modern, and visually engaging e-commerce website for a coloring book brand called “Pinta Panda.”

BRAND OVERVIEW:
Pinta Panda is a creative coloring brand offering high-quality coloring books and printable coloring experiences for both kids and adults. The brand should feel joyful, imaginative, cozy, artistic, and welcoming to people of all ages.

The website should appeal to two main audiences:
1. Kids and parents looking for fun, educational, and creative coloring books.
2. Adults looking for relaxing, mindful, artistic, and stress-relieving coloring experiences.

DESIGN DIRECTION:
Create a premium but playful visual identity. The website should not feel overly childish because adults are also an important audience.

Visual style:
- Clean and modern layout
- Soft, cheerful color palette
- Panda-inspired branding elements
- Hand-drawn illustrations and subtle doodles
- Rounded cards and buttons
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Plenty of whitespace
- Friendly and creative typography
- A warm, cozy, artistic atmosphere

Use playful accent colors such as soft yellow, pastel pink, sky blue, lavender, mint, and warm cream. Balance these with clean white backgrounds and dark text for readability.

The panda mascot can appear throughout the website in subtle and charming ways, such as holding crayons, reading a coloring book, painting, or relaxing.
